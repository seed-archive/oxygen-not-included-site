# Story traits

**Does the seed choose a world's story traits?** No: the game's settings do; the seed then decides how the world around them is made.

## The stories

The Gravitas stories a world can hold: the Critter Manipulator, the Fossil Hunt, the hijacked Headquarters, the Lonely Minion, the Mega Brain Tank and the Morb Rover Maker.

## On the map

The buildings a world is generated with — lockers, vending machines, the Neural Vacillator, the Anti Entropy Thermo-Nullifier and the rest — are marked on the [seed map](/tools/map).
