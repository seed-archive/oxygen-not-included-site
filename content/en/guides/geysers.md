# Geysers

**How much does a geyser really give?** Its long-term average: the mass it emits a cycle while active, times the share of time it is active — far less than the eruption you see.

## Eruption, rest and dormancy

A geyser erupts for part of each period and rests for the rest of it; over a longer stretch it is active, then dormant for many cycles. The game rolls each of these for every geyser from the seed and its place, within the ranges of its type in the [catalogue of geysers](/catalog/geysers).

## The average, and the tank

The average is what a colony can count on. A building that draws the average without stopping needs a tank that holds the average times the dormancy; pumps and pipes must take the eruption's flow. The [seed map](/tools/map) gives all three on every geyser's card.

## What it feeds

- Clean water: an electrolyzer takes {{electrolyzerWater}} kg/s and gives {{electrolyzerOxygen}} kg/s of oxygen at {{electrolyzerWatts}} W, enough for {{electrolyzerDuplicants}} duplicants breathing {{duplicantOxygen}} kg/s each.
- Natural gas: a generator burns {{gasBurn}} kg/s for {{gasWatts}} W.
- Hydrogen: a generator burns {{hydrogenBurn}} kg/s for {{hydrogenWatts}} W.
