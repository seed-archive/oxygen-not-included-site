# Choosing a seed

**What makes an Oxygen Not Included seed good?** Water, oxygen and power you can reach early, counted as the long-term average of the geysers, not the eruption you see first.

## Count the average, not the eruption

A geyser erupts for part of each period and rests between, and it falls dormant for whole stretches of cycles. What it gives a colony is its average over all of that: the mass per cycle times the share of the year it is active. The [seed map](/tools/map) shows that average on every geyser's card, with the tank that carries a steady draw through its dormancy.

## What a seed can feed

Clean water feeds electrolyzers, and their oxygen feeds duplicants; natural gas and hydrogen run generators. Each geyser's card on the map says how many. Polluted and salt water feed them too once cleaned.

## Where things are

A start close to the surface shortens the way to space; a start near water, algae and dirt shortens the first cycles. The map draws every cell of the asteroid in the game's colours, so the biomes, the abyssalite between them and the magma at the bottom read at a glance.
