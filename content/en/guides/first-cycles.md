# First cycles

**What does a new colony need from its world?** Algae or water for oxygen, dirt for food, and both close to the Printing Pod.

## Oxygen

A duplicant breathes {{duplicantOxygen}} kg/s. An oxygen diffuser turns {{diffuserAlgae}} kg/s of algae into {{diffuserOxygen}} kg/s of oxygen, enough for {{diffuserDuplicants}} duplicants, until the algae runs out; an electrolyzer on a water source lasts.

## Food

A duplicant eats {{duplicantKcal}} kcal a cycle. Mealwood gives {{mealwoodKcal}} kcal every {{mealwoodCycles}} cycles and eats {{mealwoodDirt}} kg of dirt a cycle, so {{mealwoodPerDuplicant}} plants feed one duplicant.

## On the map

The [seed map](/tools/map) draws every cell of the start asteroid, so the algae, the dirt and the water around the Printing Pod show where they are.
